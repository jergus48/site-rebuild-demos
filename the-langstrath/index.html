Stonethwaite · Borrowdale Valley

Two great walks pass our door.

Set at the bottom of the Langstrath valley, away from the main road, the inn sits on Wainwright's Coast to Coast and the Cumbrian Way, and makes an ideal base for England's highest peak, Scafell Pike.
