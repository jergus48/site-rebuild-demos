Deepdale Hall is a grade II listed 17th century farmhouse on a working Lakeland hill farm, in the scenic valley of Deepdale, close to Patterdale, between Lake Ullswater and Brotherswater, in one of the most beautiful parts of the Lake District National Park.

  • The ByreA luxury self-catering barn conversion for 4, with a double and a twin, an open plan living room with a woodburning stove, and glass doors onto private seating facing the fells.
  • Shepherds HutsFour insulated timber huts with wifi, cotton towels and linen, an electric heater and outside seating.
  • The farmA working hill farm, with stunning mountain views all around.
