The gallery

Three rooms, one courtyard.

One of England's longest established independent retail craft galleries, known for individuality, varied choice and careful selection.

Opposite the Shakespeare Hotel, three adjoining rooms open onto a courtyard that floods them with natural light. Expect a warm welcome, knowledgeable staff and attentive service.
