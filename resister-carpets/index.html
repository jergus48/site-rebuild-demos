About Resister Carpets

Resister Carpets are based in High Wycombe, a short drive from M40 Junction 3 and 4 and then along the A40. We sell carpets, laminate flooring and wooden flooring at trade prices, direct to the public.

We are always having clearouts and sales, so we aim to be unbeatable on price in and around the area. You are welcome to visit our store to see what we have and what we can do for you.
