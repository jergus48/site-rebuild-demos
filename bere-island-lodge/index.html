Off the ferry.

The Lodge is a few yards from the quay, in the heart of Lawrence Cove.

Blackberries and butterflies.

"I love the blackberries and butterflies." A favourite line from our guest book.

Seven miles of history.

Standing stones and megalithic cairns, Martello towers and old forts, across an island seven miles long.

Once the Coal Yard.

In my father's and grandfather's time, the site of the Lodge was the Coal Yard, where the British Army kept coal for its barracks. In 2002, Edel and I built The Lodge here, purpose designed for today's visitors to the island.

  • RoomsFamily rooms, doubles, twins, singles and small dormitories, all en suite. One double has special needs facilities, and the Lodge is wheelchair friendly throughout.
  • Breakfast or self-cateringA Bere Island breakfast, or full use of the kitchen, dining room, lounge, laundry and drying room.
  • On the doorstepMary's shop across the road, Dessie's pub a few steps on, and the marina five minutes away.
  • Getting outBike hire, fishing trips, and a guided walk every Saturday at 9.30am.
  • Getting hereTwo licensed car ferries, from Castletownbere, or Murphy's Ferry Services from the Pontoon.
