The McCaul family, from the quarries of Mount Charles to Pearse Road, Letterkenny.

A family of stonemasons.

The McCaul family came from Mount Charles, where they helped quarry the stone for St. Eunan's Cathedral. Tony McCaul moved to Letterkenny in 1937 and established McCaul Memorials, and his son, also Tony, carried on the business after his father's death in 1964.
