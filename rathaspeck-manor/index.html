A house with a long memory.

The Codd family, who came to Ireland around 1169, built Rathaspeck between 1680 and 1720, near the site of a castle Sir Osborne Codd raised here in 1351. The Meyler family came in 1911, and from them the Manor passed to the Cuddihy family.
