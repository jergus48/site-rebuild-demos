Jimmy to James.

The Nolans have been stone carvers for five generations. Jimmy Nolan skilfully hand carved memorials, and when he retired he handed the business to his son James, who keeps that tradition and has branched out into building stone and more.

  • Building stone and paversFor walls, buildings and patios.
  • Multifuel stovesQuality cast iron, for wood, logs and coal. Compact models without a boiler, or with one that heats up to 20 radiators.
