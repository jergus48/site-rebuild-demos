Come and go as you please.

A family-run bed and breakfast with 24/7 access and no curfew, a few minutes' walk from the seafront, Ocean Beach Pleasure Park and King Street. Breakfast is freshly cooked to order from 7am, seven days a week.

Getting here

Metro, A1 or two wheels.

South Shields is on the Tyne and Wear Metro, and we're close to the A1, A19 and the Tyne Tunnel. Free parking for cars, ample space for vans, and free overnight parking for bicycles and motorcycles.
