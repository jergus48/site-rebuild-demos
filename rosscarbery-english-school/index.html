Small town, big welcome.

Rosscarbery is a coastal town with its own Blue Flag beach, and a perfect base for the scenery, history and culture of West Cork. The school combines English lessons, afternoon activities and host families into one complete educational holiday.

  • English lessonsThe core of the programme.

  • The townA Blue Flag beach, restaurants, sports facilities and traditional music sessions.
  • GroupsProgrammes for groups of 10 or more, planned with you.
