Since 1987

Derby's record shop.

Dave Hill, with former business partner Lisa Woolley, opened BPM in Derby in 1987 after working in music sales since the 1970s, managing stores including HMV and R.E.Cords. With the iconic Designers Republic logo, BPM reopened in 2010 and has settled into its shop over two floors in the Old Blacksmiths Yard.

Our BPM tote bags are available in store for £5.00, lightweight with a comfortable over the shoulder strap, ideal for carrying your records.
