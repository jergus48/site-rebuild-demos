Two brothers bought an old flax mill in Longford in 1913 and started sawing. The mill has not stopped since.

Measured.

Every log becomes a plan: strength graded carcassing, CLS, pallet wood, decking and fencing.

Cut.

450,000 cubic metres of sawn wood a year across the group, and 220,000 of kiln drying.

Home.

Timber frame homes and engineered roof trusses. A log goes in, a house comes out.

Water, steam, oil, then electric in 1947.

William and James Glennon set up the sawmill after returning from the United States. William ran it until 1943, his son Paddy led it for over 50 years, and Paddy's sons Pat and Mike became joint managing directors in the mid 1990s.
