At the heart of the village.

The Well Parc stands in almost two acres of grounds overlooking miles of glorious coastline, with Trevose Head to the left and the famous Round Hole of Trevone to the right, less than five minutes' walk from the beaches and coastal path. It is also a village inn at the heart of Trevone life, supporting local and national charities.
