Trevone · near Padstow · Cornwall

Village inn, coast on the doorstep.

A family-run hotel in almost two acres overlooking miles of coastline, with Trevose Head to the left and the Round Hole of Trevone to the right. The beaches and coastal path are under five minutes' walk.

The family

Oxford to Trevone, 1980.

Ray, Sally and Lee moved down from Oxford in 1980 and started at Well Parc with Harry, Joan and Greg. Lee and Jodie are now taking over together, with family still helping in the kitchen. Trevone's two unspoilt beaches are often missed by the summer rush.
