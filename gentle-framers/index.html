Three generations

A trade handed down.

1950

The family's framing history begins, the craft passed from grandfather to Basil.

1972

Basil Gentle opens The Gentle Gallery on Steyning High Street, and Nicholas joins the firm.

1995

Nicholas is awarded the Fine Art Trade Guild's Commended Framer Award.

2007

After 35 years in Steyning, Nicholas and Margaret open the workshop at Sandhill Barn.

Today

A small family business with over 60 years of framing history.
