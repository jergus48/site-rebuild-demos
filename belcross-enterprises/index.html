Belcross Enterprises Ltd was established in Belmullet in 1999. Before that, its directors ran Belcross Haulage in the UK from 1991 to 1999, working on major road and infrastructure projects around London. A high proportion of the company's work comes from recommendations from satisfied clients.

  • ConstructionDomestic extensions and renovations to commercial projects, with qualified staff and long-standing sub-contractors.
  • SafetyAll sites managed under HSE management systems, and every employee holds the required HSE training.
  • PlantEnough of our own plant to run projects efficiently, and specialist equipment when needed.
  • SurveyingGPS and GNSS surveying on land and marine, and all types of setting out.
  • MarineFully certified and insured vessels with crews qualified to Department of Marine standards, for multi-national clients including the Shell E&P Ireland project.
