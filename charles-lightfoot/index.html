History

Over a century in church glazing.

Lightfoots first started trading in 1890. Managing Director Geoff Thornton has been restoring and producing stained glass windows and decorative leaded lights for over 30 years, and the studio is commissioned for restoration work around the country, completed with innovation and flair.
