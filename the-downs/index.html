A seafront tradition since before the war.

The Downs has offered serviced accommodation since before the Second World War, in a Victorian terrace built around 1850, believed to have been built by Isaac Singer of sewing machine fame. Kate and Ed Wood welcome you to their warm, friendly guest house.
