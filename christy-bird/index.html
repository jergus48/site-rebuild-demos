Nearly eighty years in furniture.

Christy Bird had been manager of the pawnbroker on Charlemont Mall. When he retired in 1945 he bought a shop on South Richmond Street, Portobello, and it became one of Dublin's best known antique and second-hand shops. Today it makes bespoke furniture with lots of character, functional, durable and beautiful.
