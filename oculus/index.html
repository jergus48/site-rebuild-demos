Our story

From one corner to a boutique.

1996

Oculus starts life as a contact lens centre on the corner of All Saints Green, and Jeff and his team grow it into one of the leading contact lens opticians in the Eastern region.

2014

The concept of Oculus Norwich is born, inspired by a boutique optician in Glasgow.

2016

The practice moves to 16 All Saints Green, designed as both a boutique eyewear optician and a contact lens specialist.

Oculus is in every way a family business. The team has worked together for many years, shares the same values, invests in ongoing training and runs the business democratically.
