Our story

Gelato the way Nonna made it.

Oddono's Gelati Italiani started trading in July 2004 on Bute Street, South Kensington. It was set up by Christian Oddono, whose grandmother made gelato at home in Italy.

Every batch is still produced daily in our artisan laboratory from in-house recipes, adding each ingredient individually. No colourings, preservatives or GM products, ever.
