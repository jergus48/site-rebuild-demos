Regular maintenance matters to your trees and to everything underneath them. Not taking action at the right time can lead to unnecessary problems later. Not sure what yours needs? Here is how we break it down.

  • Crown thinningFor an overgrown crown that catches the wind like a sail. Branches are thinned to cut the wind load and the risk of failure.
  • Tree overhaulFor deadwood, disease, broken or split branches. The tree is cleared of them so it can keep growing safely.
  • Deadwood removalDead limbs are weak, and strong wind brings them down. We survey, find and remove them.
  • Crown reductionFor a top that has become too heavy.
  • FellingUsually a last resort, for dead, diseased or damaged trees.
  • Stump removalSpecialised grinders for stumps of any size, even in narrow spots.
  • Hedges and site clearanceSmall garden hedges to long boundaries, and sites cleared for development, roots and all.
