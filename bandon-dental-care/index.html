Treatments

Prevention first, everything else too.

Oral health screening

Every tooth, the supporting gums and a full mouth cancer screening. Under-16s get an examination and preventative advice.

Hygienist visits

Half-hour visits for gum disease, stain removal and polishing, with hygiene, diet and smoking cessation advice.

Fillings

Silver amalgam or tooth-coloured composite, whichever suits your tooth and your preference.

Root canal

Front teeth, premolars and molars, x-ray included in the fee.

Crowns, bridges, veneers

Materials chosen for function and the look you want.

Dentures

Full and partial, plus relines and repairs.
