Seventy fishermen, one meeting, 1920.

About 70 interested fishermen met at the Town Hall on 14 January 1920. On 9 February the United Fishermen's Co-operative Society (St Ives) Ltd was formed.

People who lived in Downlong have recalled an Aladdin's Cave: oilskins, leather sea boots, lanterns, glass floats, ropes and nets, and the smell of paraffin and linseed oil.
