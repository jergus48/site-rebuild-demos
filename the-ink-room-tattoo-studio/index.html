Looking after it

  • First hourKeep it covered for a minimum of one hour.
  • First washWash gently with warm water and pat dry. Always wash and dry your hands before you touch it.
  • After a showerPat dry. Do not rub.
  • Two or three times a dayMoisturise to help it heal, with nothing scented.
  • About two weeksAny scabbing should be gone, then a further 10 to 14 days to heal.
  • At workKeep it covered in dirty or dusty conditions, and no picking or scratching.
