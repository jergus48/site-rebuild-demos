History

Born of a bicentenary.

In July 2009 Lewes staged a festival for the bicentenary of Tom Paine's death, and the press was on show, fully working, in the town's historic Market Tower.

It is Peter Chasseaud's project: an artist, writer and maker of artist's books who has lived in Lewes since 1973, a long-standing member of the Headstrong Club and a founder member of the Tom Paine Project in 2000. The press now shares 151 High Street with Press Gallery.
