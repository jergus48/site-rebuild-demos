The firm

A Hexham practice since 1982.

MarstonHarbottle is the successor practice of Travis Marston & Co, set up by Jane Marston and Andrew Travis in 1982. Ruth Harbottle joined in 1998 and became a partner in 2002. We are a litigation based firm handling the work people typically need through their lives, and each solicitor has her own specialities.
