Under Benwiskin.

A hostel and community centre in Ballintrillick, North Sligo, at the foot of the Gleniff Horseshoe.

It began with a clean-up.

In 1992, nine neighbours organised a clean-up day against illegal dumping in the valley. The centre grew from there.

Room for 31.

Private en suite rooms and self-catering, refurbished and extended in 2019.

Everyone's welcome.

Classes, community events, hall hire and weddings, all year round.

Built by the community.

Ballintrillick Environmental Group was formed in March 1992 to protect the local environment. They took on the old Vocational School, which had fallen into disrepair, and in July 2000 it opened as The Benwiskin Centre: a community centre that also runs as a hostel, with guests all year round.

  • Hostel and self-catering31 beds, sleeping 31 to 34 people, for individuals, couples, families and groups.
  • Hall and room hireWith or without accommodation, for meetings, classes, parties and weddings.
  • ClassesA seasonal class schedule, with workshops like jewellery making.
  • CommunitySummer camps, socials, Halloween and a Christmas craft fair.
  • OutdoorsThe Gleniff Horseshoe, beaches, walks and cycle paths, about five miles from the coast.
