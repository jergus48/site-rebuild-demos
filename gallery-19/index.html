Our story

A painter's own gallery.

Gordon French, a painter and avid collector of architectural and classical prints, opened Gallery 19 in 1995 on the corner of Thackeray Street and Kensington Court Place, a charming independent street tucked behind the High Street.

In 1996 the basement became a framing workshop, which has grown by word of mouth. We're a family business, supported by the local community from the very beginning.
