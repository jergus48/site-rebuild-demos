Our story

Three generations on this land.

Before

The land has belonged to the family for 3 generations, growing fruit and vegetables for their farm shop, Sheeplands.

20+ years

Andrew ran Sheeplands farm shop for over 20 years.

2011

Andrew Cardy started to develop Riverways Farm, with the welfare of the animals at its heart.

Today

All generations of the family are very hands on with the animals.
