A family vineyard near Canterbury, on soils that match the terroir of Champagne. It starts bare.

Then everything grows.

2½ hectares of vines, rows running up and down the slope to catch all the sun there is.

Picked by hand.

Pinot Noir, Pinot Gris, Bacchus and more, 100% grown and hand harvested on our own estate.

Pour it.

White, rosé, red and sparkling, by the bottle or half case. Double Gold, London Wine Competition 2026.

The monks had the idea in 1303.

Prior Henry of Canterbury Cathedral established Chartham's first recorded vineyard, and the Cathedral Archives still hold what it cost to keep. Ours began 710 years later, on part of a farm that has been in Roz Waller's family for 70 years.
