In the workshop

Go, stop and handle, the way it should.

Servicing

A regular service keeps on top of the small jobs that otherwise build up. At the very least, an annual oil change is well worthwhile.

Electrical work

From small repairs to complete rewires, including the 6 volt systems common on older Volkswagens.

Bodywork and restoration

Body and chassis restoration with high-quality repairs, designed to last if you are keeping the car.

Rebuilds and upgrades

Standard or modified, rebuilt and upgraded to go, stop and handle well. Twin carburettor tuning checked too.

Engine building

Careful selection and assembly of parts, allowing for the quality issues found in many modern components.

Corrosion protection

Dinitrol Waxoil treatment and undersealing, plus ultrasonic cleaning of carburettors.

Who we are

Grown up with aircooleds.

T2s, Beetles and 412s were the family's daily drivers.

Childhood

Today

Aircooled and watercooled VWs, from an annual service to a full restoration, at Station Yard in Swaffham.

A popular conversion

The Subaru swap.

Subaru conversions have become very popular in recent years, with a whole industry built up around the supply of parts. We also carry out suspension and handling work so the car keeps up.
